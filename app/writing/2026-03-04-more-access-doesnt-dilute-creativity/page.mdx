<Post title="More access to creative tools doesn't dilute creativity" date="March 4, 2026">

More access to creative tools doesn't dilute creativity. If anything, it expands it. We don't look at libraries and think "this is bad because more people can read now."

Tools like Canva or AI aren't replacing designers, they're giving more people a way to make things, to experiment, to play. That doesn't make creativity less valuable.

</Post>
